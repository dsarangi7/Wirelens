1

Open a project, pick a rack

The Projects panel (on the right, or at the top on a phone) shows each project as a folder. Open a folder to see its racks and banks, then click one to open its 3D wiring guide.

The search box finds racks in all projects at once, e.g. bank 14, Type B or service.

To come back here, click the Wirelens logo or All racks.

2

The three areas

  • Rack list (left): jump to another rack. « folds it away.
  • Cable list: every cable the customer connects in this rack.
  • 3D model: the rack with all its cables, coloured by type.

Drag the border between the list and the model to make the list wider or narrower.

3

Look around the rack

  • Mouse: drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.
  • Touch: one finger to rotate, pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan.

The buttons at the top fly the camera to a fixed view: Rear Front Signal panel MSM Overview.

4

Find a cable

Type a cable name or a port in the search box, e.g. SSC3 or EMS.

The colour chips show or hide a type of cable: AC power, communication, E-stop, customer connections and more. A chip's colour is the cable's colour in the model.

The list is grouped by device. Click a group to open it.

5

Follow the route

Pick a cable and its card opens. It shows where the cable starts and ends, the cable and connector, the harness number, what AYK supplies and which technote applies.

The numbered steps are the route. The camera flies to each one and a light runs along the cable.

  • Play route / Pause, ◀ ▶ for one step at a time, or click a step.
  • Slow Normal Fast sets the speed.
  • Keyboard: ← → for steps, Esc to close.
6

Click in the model

Point at a cable or a port to see its name.

  • Click a cable to open its route. Click another part of the same cable to jump to that step.
  • Click a port to see its connector and which cable plugs into it.
7

Front cables and the CAD model

Racks with power and communication cables on the front get Power Comm Both at the top, so you can show one set at a time.

Racks built from a SolidWorks model have a CAD model button. It switches between the real model and a simplified one.

8

Harness list

The Harnesses tab lists every cable with its harness number and route length in mm, plus the total length.

An * means the number is assumed from a labelled cable of the same kind. Please confirm those with AYK.

Download CSV saves the list for Excel. Click a row to show that cable in the model.

9

About this model

About this model says what the rack was built from and how many devices and cables it has.

It also lists the draft assumptions. Read them before you rely on a detail.

10

Share a cable

When a cable is open, the address in the browser ends with that cable's name (after the #).

Copy the address and send it to a colleague. The link opens the same rack with the same cable selected.

11

On a phone or tablet

  • ☰ Cables opens the cable list. It closes again when you pick a cable.
  • The route card sits at the bottom. ▾ shrinks it so you can see more of the rack.
  • The buttons at the top scroll sideways.

Turn the phone sideways for a bigger view.
